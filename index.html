How to read a shelf weight rating

UDL means uniformly distributed load. A 200kg UDL shelf holds 200kg only when the weight is spread across the whole shelf. The same 200kg sitting in one spot in the middle bends the shelf far more, so this calculator halves the usable rating when “Concentrated in one spot” is selected. That is a cautious rule of thumb, not a manufacturer figure.

  • Shelf rating and bay rating are separate limits. Four shelves at 200kg do not always mean 800kg for the bay; check both.
  • Heaviest items go on the lowest shelf. It keeps the centre of gravity low and makes lifting safer.
  • Tall units should be fixed to the wall when they stand on their own, especially in homes and garages with children.
  • Leave headroom. Staying below about 75% of the rating allows for heavier-than-expected boxes.
